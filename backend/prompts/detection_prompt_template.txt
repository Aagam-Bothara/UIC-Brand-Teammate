# Content Analysis Task

You are analyzing a UIC communication to identify potential issues related to brand compliance, accessibility, and quality.

## Context

**Target Audience**: {audience}
**Communication Channel**: {channel}
**Reading Level Target**: {reading_level_target}

## Text to Analyze

{text_to_analyze}

## Retrieved Brand Guidelines

Use these UIC brand guidelines as your reference:

{guidelines_context}

## Analysis Instructions

Review the text and identify issues in the following categories:

### 1. Brand Compliance Issues
Look for:
- Incorrect university name usage (should be "University of Illinois Chicago" on first reference)
- Missing or incorrect boilerplate/tagline
- Off-brand terminology or phrasing
- Inconsistent voice or tone with UIC brand attributes
- Incorrect logo or visual references

### 2. Accessibility Issues
Look for:
- Reading level above the target: {reading_level_target}
- Complex jargon without explanation
- Long, dense paragraphs
- Missing or poor heading structure
- Lack of alt text references for images
- Poor color contrast or formatting descriptions

### 3. Content Quality Issues
Look for:
- Grammar or spelling errors
- Passive voice overuse
- Wordiness or redundancy
- Unclear calls-to-action
- Missing important information
- Factual inconsistencies

### 4. Reading Level Issues
Look for:
- Vocabulary above target reading level
- Sentence complexity too high
- Technical terms without definitions
- Abstract concepts without concrete examples

### 5. Audience Tone Issues
Look for:
- Tone mismatch with {audience} audience
- Inappropriate formality level
- Missing audience-relevant context
- Focus on wrong priorities for this audience

## Output Format

For each issue found, provide:

1. **Issue Type**: One of [BRAND, ACCESSIBILITY, CONTENT, READING_LEVEL, AUDIENCE_TONE]
2. **Severity**: One of [high, medium, low]
3. **Location**: Quote the specific text segment with the issue
4. **Description**: Brief explanation of the problem
5. **Suggestion**: Recommended fix or improvement

Format your response as a JSON array:

```json
[
  {
    "issue_type": "BRAND",
    "severity": "high",
    "location": "quoted text with issue",
    "description": "explanation of the problem",
    "suggestion": "recommended fix",
    "guideline_reference": "relevant guideline if applicable"
  }
]
```

Only include actual issues you've identified. If the text is compliant in all areas, return an empty array: `[]`
