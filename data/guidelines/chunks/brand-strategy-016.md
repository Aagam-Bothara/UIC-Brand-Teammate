# Brand strategy and messaging — Brand messaging > Cultural richness

**Attributes**

A vibrant and tenacious community that reflects and engages in the communities where we live, work and serve

- Growing from and alongside diverse perspectives
- Easy access to a diverse, urban campus and the vibrancy of Chicago
- Fostering a holistic sense of identity and wellness that extends beyond your education

**Benefits**

Gain real-world experiences and prepare to navigate a global society

- Becoming more adaptable, empathetic and prepared to lead in a complex, global world
- The ability to learn, live and work, and commuting seamlessly between those worlds
- Pursuing a thriving future professionally and personally
