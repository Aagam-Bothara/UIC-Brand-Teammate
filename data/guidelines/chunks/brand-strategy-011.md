# Brand strategy and messaging — Brand messsaging

To help us communicate effectively, we’ve outlined four key themes that support the brand essence. This ensures that we’re telling the UIC story in a consistent way that reinforces the foundational idea. We build our story around these powerful themes to convey UIC’s offer and benefit (our core value proposition).

**Key themes**

- Location
- Culture
- Student success
- Innovation

UIC’s [messaging map](https://uofi.box.com/s/y6v82zkoiiu7qezyb58juvv2dho5soop) is a strategic storytelling framework that defines how we articulate the university’s value and impact. It provides direction, alignment and inspiration for anyone writing on behalf of UIC.

The messaging map starts at the center with our core value proposition, and all secondary and supporting points make that core stronger.
