# Brand strategy and messaging — Brand positioning and brand essence > Brand positioning

**Who:** For those with a relentless belief that more is possible, in their lives and the lives of others.

**What:** UIC leads the way for enduring progress for Chicago, in Chicago and beyond.

**How:** With unguarded access to a high-quality education, leading research and community impact — without barriers and without limits.

**Why:** When excellence is within reach, it doesn’t just change one life. The impact echoes across generations and communities.
