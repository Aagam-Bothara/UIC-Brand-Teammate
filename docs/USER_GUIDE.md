# UIC Brand Teammate: User Guide

UIC Brand Teammate checks a draft against UIC brand guidelines and suggests a revised version. You stay in charge: every change comes with a reason and you decide whether to keep it.

## 1. Paste your draft

Paste an email, web page, social post or other text into the draft box. The word count is shown below it.

No draft handy? Click **Try a sample ▾** and pick one. The first sample, a Career Fair email, has issues for all five rulesets.

## 2. Choose audience, channel and rulesets

- **Audience:** Students, Faculty or Staff. This sets the reading-level target and tone.
- **Channel:** Email, Website or Social Media.
- **Rulesets:** turn on the checks you want:
  - **Brand:** UIC name, style and voice ("University of Illinois Chicago", not "at Chicago").
  - **Accessibility:** descriptive links, alt text, plain formatting.
  - **Content:** clear subject, call to action, contact details.
  - **Reading Level:** sentence length and grade level for your audience.
  - **Audience Tone:** right register for the audience.

## 3. Analyze

Click **Analyze**. Results usually take a few seconds. You can cancel while it runs. If you edit the draft or settings afterwards, the results are marked out of date until you analyze again.

## 4. Review changes one ruleset at a time

The original and the revised text appear side by side. Changes are revealed one ruleset at a time, so you can see what each set of rules did. Use **Reveal next** in the reveal bar to show the next ruleset, or reveal all at once. Each ruleset has its own highlight color and label.

The compliance panel shows the brand and accessibility scores, the status (Approved, Minor Revisions Needed or Major Revisions Needed) and the open issues grouped by ruleset. Scores update as you reveal, accept or reject changes.

## 5. Click a change to see why

Click any highlighted change to open its detail: what changed, why, and the UIC guideline it is based on.

## 6. Accept or reject

In the change detail, choose **Accept** or **Reject**. Rejected changes go back to your original wording, and the scores update. You can change your mind at any time.

## 7. Ask the editor

Open **Ask the editor** to request further edits in plain language, for example:

- "Make it shorter."
- "Make the tone friendlier for students."
- "Why did you change the link text?"

## 8. Export

Use **Export** to copy the revised text (**Copy text**), download it as text, or save a PDF report with the revised text, scores, issues and changes.

## Tips

- Your draft, settings, decisions and chat are saved in this browser, so a refresh does not lose your work. Click **New draft** to start over.
- The tool checks text only. It cannot see logos, images, colors, fonts or captions, so check those yourself.
- Production notes in brackets (for example `[Banner: ...]`) are treated as text.
- Scores are guidance, not approval. Have a person review anything important before it goes out.
- Keyboard: everything works with Tab, Enter and Space; Esc closes a popover or dialog.
- If the status dot in the header shows the guideline service is offline, the app still works but guideline excerpts may be generic.
